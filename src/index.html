THE BASICS

How to play

  1. Slide: Swipe across the board or use the arrow keys (WASD works too).
  2. Merge: Matching tiles combine into one with double the value.
  3. Reach 2048: Make the goal tile, then keep playing if you like.
  4. Rethink: Undo reverses your last move; choose a different grid for a fresh challenge.

Offline by design. No ads, trackers, or account needed.
