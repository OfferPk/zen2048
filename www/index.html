How to Play Zen2048

• Controls: Swipe across the board or use the Arrow keys (or W, A, S, D) to slide tiles in all four directions.

• Merge: When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one with double the value!

• Objective: Reach the 2048 tile to win! You can continue playing indefinitely to achieve higher scores.

• Undo: Tap ↩️ Undo anytime to step backward and rethink your moves.
