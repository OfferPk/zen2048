How to play

  • Slide: swipe the board (touch or mouse drag) or press the arrow keys / W A S D. Every tile slides as far as it can.
  • Merge: two equal tiles that touch merge into one with double the value, and the value is added to your score.
  • New tiles: after every move a 2 (or sometimes a 4) appears in an empty square.
  • Goal: build the goal tile shown above the board. In Classic you can keep playing for a bigger score.
  • Journey: 24 stages with different boards, 🪨 stones that never move, and ⏱ move limits. Finish in fewer moves for more ★.
  • Undo: step back with ↩️ (unlimited in Classic, 3 per Journey stage).
  • Pause: tap ⏸ or press P / Esc. Your board is saved automatically, so you can Continue later.
